How It Works

End-to-end ML pipeline for credit risk assessment

01

Data Collection & Merging

Application records merged with credit history to build a unified applicant profile.

02

Feature Engineering

Outlier removal, skewness correction, SMOTE oversampling, and one-hot encoding applied automatically.

03

Multi-Model Training

Four classifiers are trained in parallel. The best-performing model by Recall score is selected.

04

Real-Time Prediction

Flask serves predictions in under a second. Supports IBM Watson ML deployment for cloud scale.
